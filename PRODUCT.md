# nefelha Product Context

## Register
**product** — The design serves the app (classroom quiz platform), not the identity itself.

## Users
- **Teachers** (primary): Middle/high school educators in Saudi Arabia and GCC, tech-comfortable but not tech-first, want to make class engaging without friction
- **Students** (secondary): age 11-18, joining via 4-char room code from any device, expect zero friction and clear interaction
- **School admins** (tertiary): managing deployment across classes/grades

## Product Purpose
Transform how students feel about learning. Not "make learning efficient" or "gamify quizzes." Make students *want* to show up to class. The product is the experience; game mechanics, AI, and live sessions are the means.

## Brand Tone
- **Not** corporate SaaS, startup cheerfulness, or motivational-poster energy
- **Authentic to the classroom:** direct, unadorned, slightly irreverent (Arabic street-smart, not formal)
- Student-first: speak to them, not about them
- Confidence without sales-pitch; precision without jargon

## Anti-references
- Kahoot (bright, juvenile, noise)
- Blooket (trendy colors, feels temporary)
- Generic EdTech (cream backgrounds, stock illustrations, "engage your students!")
- Corporate dashboards (cold, utilitarian)

## Strategic Principles
1. **Arabic-first, always.** Not English translated. Layout, copy, pacing assume RTL fluency.
2. **No device required per student.** Physical mode (printed board + QR) is a core differentiator, not an afterthought.
3. **Teacher as hero.** UI makes the teacher look good, never tries to replace them.
4. **One platform, every subject.** Islamic Studies to Math to English—same system, no separate purchase.
5. **Live is the default.** No "async" mode marketing. Everything assumes real-time, in-class use.

## Key Surfaces
- **Landing / Marketing:** sells the vision; Blooket-inspired nav bar is *aesthetic reference only*, not a copy
- **Join (`/join`):** student entry point; frictionless room-code entry + name; sets classroom mood
- **404 Page:** lost route; currently forgettable; opportunity to reinforce brand personality
- **Teacher Dashboard:** shows game history, quizzes, analytics; needs at-a-glance clarity
- **Live Monitor:** projector view for teacher during a game; full-screen, no chrome
